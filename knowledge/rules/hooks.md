## Measured across 66 videos (all hook videos + the 19 viral Reels)
- The hook is one sentence, finished by about **5 seconds** (median 5.2s), about 12–20 words.
- In all 43 template example videos, the hook is the first thing said, word for word, and the next line delivers on it straight away ("Let me explain", "First,", "So check this out").
