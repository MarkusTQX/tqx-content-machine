# Structure and retention patterns (62 videos, Oct 23 2026)

Sources: 43 structure cards (`knowledge/cards/structure/tplNN.md`, the example videos behind the 50 hook templates), 19 viral lesson cards Mark picked (`knowledge/cards/reference/`), and 1 teaching video (@viewcci, `knowledge/cards/teaching/retention/`).

Only one creator *taught* structure, so these patterns are scored by how often successful videos *do* them (Mark, Oct 23: "watch the videos I sent you to pick up on structure"):
- **Standard:** seen in more than half the videos
- **Common:** seen in a quarter to a half
- **Option:** seen in fewer than a quarter

## Retention moves

| Move | Seen in | Status |
|---|---|---|
| At least one open loop | 42 of 43 | Standard |
| A re-hook line mid-video | 32 of 43 template videos + 9 of 19 viral = 41 of 62 | Standard |
| Numbered items (announced count, then "one... two... three") | about 25 of 62 | Common |
| Best item saved for last, and said so ("the most important, the third thing") | about 8 of 62 | Option, but strong |
| Viewer's objection voiced out loud ("But wait, you say, I don't have time") | about 6 of 62 | Option |
| Ends on a CTA | 18 of 43 | Common |

| Loops back to the first frame for a rewatch | 2 of 43 + 3 of 19 | Option |
| Visual change | every 0.7–4s; most every 1–3s | Standard |
| Pace | 2.6–4.9 words a second; middle about 3.5 | Standard |

## Re-hook lines actually used (template videos)

"And the most important, the third thing you want to do" · "But here's the shit part." · "But that's not the crazy part." · "And here's the kicker." · "Here's the science behind what's actually happening." · "So how does this work?" · "But why does that matter?" · "What a lot of people don't tell you is" · "Let that sink in." · "I've got one more for you." · "And then let's close with two more" · "I bet people would pay for this, but I'm going to show you for free." · "But wait, Paul, you say, I don't have time to make this healthy food." · "But coach, how do I get a heavy dumbbell onto my legs...?" · "Okay, what is a reference group?" · "Do you think people see you as a 10 out of 10 or a 3 out of 10?"

## What @viewcci teaches (1 creator)
1. Don't give the full answer to the hook straight away.
2. Before answering, open another question or hold the answer back.
3. Re-hook: remind them what they're waiting for, or give a second reason to stay.
4. End right after the payoff.

How this fits the data: the videos start moving toward the answer at once (a first step, a first item), but hold the full payoff back to about 80%. "Start, don't finish."
