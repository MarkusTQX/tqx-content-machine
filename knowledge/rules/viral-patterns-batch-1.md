## 3. Writing style

- **Calm insider beats hype.** Most winners sound like someone letting you in on something ("Most people don't realize…", "here's what makes this different"), not a hype man.
- **"You" in the hook, "I" for the proof.** "You need to start night trading" → "I put together a guide…".
- **Sentences of 10–17 words**, said fast. Short punch lines between them ("Completely free." "Open source and free." "Slow down.").
- **Phrases that recur:** "Most people don't realize", "here's what makes this different", "here's where it gets crazy", "the part nobody's talking about", "this is where it gets interesting", "In other words", "completely free", "I shouldn't even be doing this, but".
- **The name, the price or the answer is held back** a few seconds to create a gap (aiwithremy names the skill 10s after promising it; Hedgia's price waits until step 4).

## 4. Product placement: three models

| Model | Where the product is | Share of video | Reels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The product is the video** | Named in the first 8s, as the answer to the hook | 60–95% | daniel.cross.ai, ai.honeycove, julias.algos ×2, aiwithremy |
| **One slot in a stack** | Each tool gets one numbered job ("#1 connect…, #2 connect…") | 30–40% each | samin_yasar12, ai.with.andrew, max_kelleyy |
| **Lead magnet at the end** | Only in the CTA: a guide, a portfolio, a community | 5–10% | gingertrading01, joe.investss, raycfu, svermm |

None of the winners hides the product until the last two lines when the product *is* the topic. Our "last two lines" rule only matches the lead-magnet model.

## 5. Build-up and reward

- **Numbered items create small loops.** "#1… #2…", "Level 1… 5", "4 steps" keep people waiting for the next number.
- **A second hook at 60–75%** in 9 of 19: "here's where it gets crazy", "the part nobody's talking about", "this is where it gets interesting", "the best part".
- **The reward lands at 60–80%,** and the CTA offers the deeper version of the same reward (the guide, the repo links, the prompt). The CTA feels like part of the payoff, not an ad.
- **Weak rewards cost them:** svermm's ending contradicts his hook; ai.with.andrew never shows the alert arriving; joe.investss gives no action step.

## 6. CTAs

- **Name exactly what they'll get:** "comment AI and I'll send you over the repo links plus a setup guide", "Comment signal, and I'll send you my full video showing you exactly how to build it", "Comment Kronos and I'll DM you the link".
- **The keyword matches the topic** (Kronos, Hedgia, skill, firm, signal, quant). Comments then run at 40–265% of likes.
- **Scarcity line:** "I shouldn't even be doing this, but…" (17,827 comments).
- **Play the CTA over footage of the reward** (samin_yasar12 shows the tutorial while asking).
- **No-product videos use a share CTA aimed at one kind of friend:** "Send this to a friend who loves to trade as soon as the market opens".
- **"Like the video and comment…"** appears in 2 of 19.

## 7. Visuals

- Captions 1–3 words at a time, centred. Key words in a second colour.
- The proof is on screen from frame 1: the chart, the GitHub page, the paper title, the UI.
- Split screen (presenter + screen) or a top-third insert; never more than 3s without something new.
- Real screens beat graphics as proof: GitHub stars, the product UI, a paper's title and authors.

| "Claude copies insider trades" (never shown) | Overclaims | Only show what Claude → Qube or GATC actually does on screen |
| "$70 million of sales advice" | Unproven credential | Use a real, sourced number |
| "Just open-sourced, free on GitHub" | Not true for Qube | "Free to start, no card required" is true |
