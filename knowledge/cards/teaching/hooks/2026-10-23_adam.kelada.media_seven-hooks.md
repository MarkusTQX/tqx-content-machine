## Rules taught (template, then his example)
| # | Template | Example |
| 1 | "[X] years of [niche] in 60 seconds" | "Three years of therapy in 60 seconds" |
| 2 | "Never start an X if you don't have these Y things" | "Never start a business if you don't have these four things" |
| 3 | "I'm going to X for Y days straight" | "I'm going to study eight hours..." |
| 4 | "Rating X so you don't waste your Y" | "Rating supplements so you don't waste your money" |
| 5 | "These are the [N] best ways to use X" | "These are the five best ways to use these sound effects" |
| 6 | "This is exactly what you want to see when you're X" | "...when you're buying an industrial prop" |
| 7 | "If you take/do B every single day, you're going to X, Y and Z" | "If you take creatine every single day you're going to build more muscle, recover faster..." |

## Conflicts and doubts
- #7 is a promised result. In finance it becomes a return promise (`finance-compliance`): rejected for TQX unless the result is a labelled test.
