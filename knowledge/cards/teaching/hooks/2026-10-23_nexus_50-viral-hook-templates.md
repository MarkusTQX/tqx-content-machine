## Conflicts and doubts
- Several templates promise big measurable results (5, 27, 29, 36, 41, 44). In finance that's a promised return unless it's a real, labelled test (`finance-compliance`). The TQX column keeps the shape and drops the promise.
- Credential templates (24, 49, 50) only work if the number or title is true.
- No evidence is given for any template beyond one example video each.
