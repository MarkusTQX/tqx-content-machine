## Rules taught
| # | Their words (quoted) | The rule in one plain line | Example they gave | Evidence they gave | Time |
| 1 | "Stop using hooks in your videos like, here's my ultimate hack to steal my secret strategy" | Don't use worn-out power words ("ultimate hack", "secret strategy", "illegal") | "Here are three illegal websites" | Opinion: "everyone's heard these power words a million times" | 0:00 |
| 2 | "the viral video used a power word that was unique within its context" | Use one power word that's unusual for the topic | "disgustingly easy" instead of "super easy"; "mind-numbingly simple" instead of "super simple" | Two of his own videos: 7K vs 1.2M views | 0:26 |
| 3 | (shown, not said) | Name the specific outcome and timeframe instead of a generic list | "If you want to melt off body fat this summer, this is exactly what you should do" (1.2M) vs "Here are the three secrets to fat loss" (7K) | Same two videos | 0:16 |
