## What keeps you watching (observations tied to timestamps, 2–4 bullets)
- 0-8s: the funnel of numbers (1,000 → 50% useless → 25% wrong → 11) makes the final list feel rare and pre-filtered.
- 8-34s: no explanation per item, just name and thumbnail every ~2s, so the list moves faster than attention can drop.
- 30s: "let's close with two more" tells the viewer the end is near, which keeps them for the last items.
- 34-40s: the list lives in the caption, and the last line frames not learning from them as hard work, a challenge rather than a sign-off.
