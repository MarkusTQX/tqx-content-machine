- The demo is 3 steps, each a separate screen with the pen pointing (17.3s, 18.9s, 21.8s, 24.5s): find, click, mimic.
- "Waiting for him to close" (28.4s) compresses an hour into 2 seconds and builds tension before the result.

- "I don't even know what candlesticks are" (32.6s) makes it feel possible for anyone, right before the CTA.
