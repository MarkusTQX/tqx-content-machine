## Why it works (observations, not guesses)
- The first spoken words are a specific, named trade with an absurd result ("Micron... 138 million percent", 0–7s), and the title says the same number, so the number lands twice in 3 seconds.
- The flip at 7.0s ("imagine you did the exact opposite") doubles the surprise with zero new setup. Same stock, same trade, reversed, -99%.
- "Micron isn't the only stock" at 13.6s turns a fluke into a pattern and adds a named journal, which answers the doubt the hook created.
- The open loop is said out loud at 20s ("So what's actually going on?"), right before the payoff, so viewers who are about to swipe stay.
- The explanation is a simple "two crowds" story (20.5s) instead of finance terms. No jargon in the whole script.
- A new graphic or text card arrives about every 2 to 4s (0–38s) even though there are only 5 hard cuts, so the frame keeps changing.
- Keyword CTA "comment stocks" (38.2s) plus a big dollar number on screen ($73,467) gives a reason to comment; 344 comments.
