## Why it works (observations, not guesses)
- Every answer is hidden under one black box from frame one (0s). The viewer can see six blanks and wants all six.
- One number lands every 5 to 8 seconds (3s, 7.4s, 16.6s, 23.6s, 29.3s, 50.8s), so there's always a reveal coming.
- The biggest number comes first (12,000 at 3s), which sets a shock anchor for everything after.
- The last item is set up as the hardest to win ("compete against other people fiercely", 37.8s) and its number is held to the very last second (50.8s).
- The product plug (18.1s) is attached to a number on screen ("cut this down by 300 to 400 hours"), so it feels like part of the answer.
- The woman in pyjamas walking behind him (6–14s) adds an unplanned, human moment right where the first viewers would swipe.
- The single take with no edits (0–51s) reads as low-effort and honest.
