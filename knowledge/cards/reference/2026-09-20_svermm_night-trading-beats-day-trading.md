## Why it works (observations, not guesses)
- The first three words are a command that sounds wrong ("you need to start night trading", 0s) and the title backs it with ❌/✅ emojis. The viewer has to stay for the reason.
- "No, seriously" (1.4s) lands on the first cut, right as the chart appears, so the second beat feels like proof.
- The absurd number arrives inside 9s and is shown as a chart before it is said (chart at 1.4s, number spoken at about 6s).
- "this isn't even the only stock" (14.5s) plus a 9-chart grid of world indices (16.8s) makes it feel universal in one image.
- "Now, why is this even a thing?" at 25s says the open loop out loud just before the answer.
- A direct share prompt naming a type of friend ("that one friend who always tends to lose money", 47.2s). The likes at 1.32x followers suggest reach well past his own audience, though shares aren't visible.
- His energy and constant smile (every frame) make a dry stat feel like gossip.

## What we can't copy, and the honest TQX version
| In the original | Why we can't | TQX version |
|---|---|---|
| "you need to start night trading" | Tells viewers to trade a strategy with no test (`finance-compliance`: never tell viewers to go live before testing) | "Before you try night trading, test it. Here's what happened when I built it in Qube." |
| "you'd be up 138 million percent on paper" | Unsourced number; implied return | A Qube backtest of the same rule on one stock, costs on, with "Backtest, not live results", the period and the number of trades on screen |
| "you're statistically more likely to make profit" | A profit promise built from an unnamed paper | Name the paper on screen and say what it found, in its words. No "you'll profit". |
| "see my trades in real time" / "Join the free group in my bio for all of my plays!" | "Copy my trades" offer; TQX stands against tip groups and gurus (`positioning`) | "You don't need anyone's plays. Type your idea into Qube and test it yourself." CTA keyword QUBE |
| Ending on "time in the market" with a chart that doesn't match the topic | Mismatched proof; the hook and the lesson contradict each other | Land on one difference: "Test it before you risk it." End on the labeled backtest result, win or lose |
| Index chart grid with no source | Unsourced numbers | Use only charts with the source on screen, or our own labeled Qube backtests |
