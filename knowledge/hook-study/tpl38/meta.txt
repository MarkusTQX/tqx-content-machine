1. Google Vanguard, Fidelity, or Schwab
2. Click Open an Account
3. Go to dashboard → transfer → set up monthly recurring deposits
4. Buy shares of S&P funds each month and dollar cost average
5. Max out your Roth IRA for the next 40 years and you’ll have $3M 😮

Investing early is crucial. The sooner you start, the more time your money has to benefit from compound interest.
