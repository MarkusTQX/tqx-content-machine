where you came from, so this is your backstory, condensed down to the most important parts,
where you're going, which is essentially what you're pursuing currently. Did you just move
to LA to pursue acting, or maybe your dream is to open up a coffee shop. Either way,
you need to express what journey you are on compared to where you want to be. And number
three is what you care about. These are the values that you live by, the daily practices and habits
that you have that inform the life that you live and the content that you make. Now, once you share
your story, there are three kinds of people that you will attract. Number one are the people that
will resonate with you. Whether you come from a similar background or you have similar goals,
I can guarantee you that there are hundreds, thousands, and millions of people that will
resonate with your story. Number two are the people that look up to you and learn from you.
Even if you feel like you are completely lost in life, I guarantee you there are still people
that wish they were in your position. Let's say you just moved to LA and you're pursuing acting,
right? Maybe you haven't gotten a single gig yet, but you can still talk to all of those people
who haven't yet made it out to LA. You are always an expert to somebody else. Remember that.
Now, these two groups of audiences become your core community. This audience allows you to build
the leverage and the platform and the credibility that you need to be able to generate income. Now,
the third group of people are the people that notice you and have the power to invest in you. These
are often ordinary viewers that fall under the other two buckets, but they also happen to work in
influencer marketing or they're an investor of some kind. They will be the ones that will notice your
community, the leverage, and the credibility that you have, and they will bring you those external
opportunities such as brand deals or collaborations. So these two things combined, the people who make up
your community and the people who see potential in you and your journey and where you're going,
