Do you want to lose weight or do you want to lose fat? If you just want to lose weight that's easy
because to lose weight calories are all that really matter. So stop lifting weights and focus
on cardio since it burns almost twice as many calories. Then cut your calorie intake as low
as possible and avoiding carbs will help the scale fall even quicker. If you do that you'll
lose weight fast but a lot of that weight will probably just come from water and muscle with a
little bit of fat. Because of that your metabolism will slow down. You'll end up with more of a
skinny fat look and any weight you do lose you'll probably gain right back as soon as you stop
dieting. Which friendly reminder you can't do forever. Now if you want to lose fat calories still
matter but they're not all that matters because there's a big difference between burning calories
and getting fit. Focus instead on lifting heavy weights to get stronger and actually consider
limiting your cardio if it makes up most of the time you exercise. Then eat in a small calorie deficit
no more than 25 percent and make sure you're getting enough protein. There's a free calculator
for both in my bio. But aiming to lose fat not just weight will give you a leaner defined
more tone look actually strengthen your metabolism. And yeah it's going to take a little bit longer.
But this time it'll actually last.
