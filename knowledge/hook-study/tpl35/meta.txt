Strawberry Mango Protein Smoothie
650 Cal | 90g Protein | $3.50/serving

- 1/2 cup frozen strawberry
- 1/2 cup frozen mango
- 1/2 cup frozen banana
- 1 + 1/2 cup nonfat greek yogurt
- splash of fairlife milk
- 2 scoops vanilla protein powder (i use kaged)

Blend and enjoy 🫶
