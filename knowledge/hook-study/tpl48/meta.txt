description: One of the biggest real estate scams happens when the owner of a home uses a will to transfer ownership to their kids or beneficiaries. If they pass away, the home still goes through court and probate which takes a huge cut based on the gross value of the property. A living trust is the best way to finalize the transfer of ownership directly to beneficiaries without going through probate. Homeowners who want their kids to inherit the home should also secure term insurance so that if they pass away before it’s paid off, their kids won’t have to pay the rest of the mortgage.
