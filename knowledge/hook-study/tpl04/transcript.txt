I really hope not the first person to tell you this, but when you're a new manager, the one-on-ones with your boss should be like this.
First, you tell them, here are the things I've accomplished, so they know that, because they're never going to know you've accomplished stuff.
The second is you want to tell them all the questions you have and issues you've run into, so they can block and tackle for you.
And the most important, the third thing you want to do, is tell them, here are my specific priorities in order.
And then you want to ask them specifically, how would you adjust my priorities?
