**The paper:** Mebane T. Faber, "A Quantitative Approach to Tactical Asset Allocation" (2007), free on SSRN. Its rule: buy when the monthly closing price is above its 10-month simple moving average, sell and hold cash when it's below, checked once a month at the last close. Only this rule is used. No number from the paper is said or shown.
**What it's tested on:** SPY (the S&P 500 fund) if Qube has it. If not, the 10 biggest US stocks: AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN, GOOGL, META, TSLA, AVGO, JPM, BRK.B (J020's set). Neither is said out loud; the backtest screen shows it.
