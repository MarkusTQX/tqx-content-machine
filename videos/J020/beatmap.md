## The engine we keep

1. Tool named in the first word, money at stake: "Claude can now test… before you risk a single dollar." Promise shown on screen by 2s, buy button locked.
2. Reveal at about 5s: what makes it possible, with a count ("11 skills… each one handles a different job" → "four rules. Each one handles a different job").
3. The smaller parts, one every 3 to 5s, with a progress bar across the top and the best part greyed out at the far right.
4. "But the craziest one…" re-hook at about 50%, kept in the split with the best tab outlined, then a full-screen cut where a number counts up from 0.
5. "So you get to see… before putting your money on the line." (cut in round 2: the hook already makes this promise, and the screen cuts back to split with "Go live" still locked)
6. The honesty line in a sudden face close-up (the only close-up).
7. The comment keyword, last.
