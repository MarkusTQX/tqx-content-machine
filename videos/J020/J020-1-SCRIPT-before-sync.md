## Script

2. One rule scans the 10 biggest US stocks looking for a drop.

3. Another caps each stock at $2,000.

4. Then an entry rule buys the day one of them drops 3%.

5. And an exit rule takes profit at 3%, or cuts your losses at 5%.

6. But the craziest part is the backtest.

7. Qube takes your rules and runs them through five years of real prices.

8. Say I'd put in $10,000. 607 trades later, it was up about $8,100.

9. At its worst point, it was down about $3,200.

10. Of course, it's a backtest, not live results, and we already know these stocks took off.
