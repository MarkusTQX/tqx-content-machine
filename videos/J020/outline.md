## Explain it to a friend (3 sentences)
1. Problem: Daniel knows he should sell at a set point, but once the trade is open he doesn't, and he's never tested an idea before risking money on it.
2. What we show: Joshua types one idea into Claude, Qube turns it into four exact rules (buy, sell, stop-loss, size), then runs them on five years of prices: 607 trades, up about $8,100 on $10,000, worst point down about $3,200, with the hindsight caveat said out loud.
3. What he walks away with: a rule he can type tonight with every exit decided before he buys, and the order to follow: backtest, then paper trading, then real money.
