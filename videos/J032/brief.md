## The race (real, or it doesn't go in)

The original's 9:18 vs 0:01 is staged. Ours is filmed for real, both runs, with the real elapsed time on screen (`claims-and-proof`: product results only from real screen recordings).
1. By hand: Joshua looks up the next earnings date for each of 10 watchlist stocks and types each one into Google Calendar. Real stopwatch running. The editor can time-skip, but the clock shows the true time and the counter shows how many are done ("3 of 10"…).
2. With Claude: one prompt ("Find the next earnings date for every stock on this list and make me a calendar file I can import into Google Calendar"). Claude pulls the dates through Alpha Vantage's earnings calendar and builds a .ics calendar file; Joshua imports it into Google Calendar. The stopwatch runs from Enter until the 10 events show in the calendar, import included, and ends on "10 of 10" and the filled calendar. (Producer's decision, Oct 27: Anthropic's Google Calendar connector is read-only, so Claude doesn't write the events itself.)
3. **Checked (Oct 27):** Anthropic's Google Calendar connector is read-only: "Claude reads your calendar only; it can't create or change events or send invitations" (https://claude.com/docs/connectors/google/calendar). So the race uses a calendar file Joshua imports, and the connector is used to read the 10 events back. The free Alpha Vantage key allows 25 requests a day (https://www.alphavantage.co/support/), enough for one take of #1 and one of the race; film them on different days so a retake fits.
4. Whatever the two times are, those are the numbers. Never change a time to make the gap bigger.
