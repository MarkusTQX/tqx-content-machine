**Hook.** I ranked 6 AI chatbots for trading from S to F, so you don't have to.

## Script

1. Quant trading just means trading with rules and data instead of your gut.

2. And yes, I am biased because we built one.

3. Asking ChatGPT what to buy. F tier. You're just getting an opinion that nobody has actually tested.

4. Gemini. D tier. It's built into Google, so it can pull up the latest news on a stock. But ask it to test a strategy, and it can hand you a profit number without running a single trade. Your results look amazing, but they're fake.

5. Grok. C tier. It lives inside X, which is where a lot of traders post. So it's quick on what people are saying about a stock. But what people are saying isn't a tested strategy.

6. Perplexity. B tier. Really good research, and it shows you where every answer came from. But to test your strategy on past prices, you're on your own.

7. Claude. A tier. Very powerful for building strategies, and it'll even write the code. But to test that code on years of prices, you have to set it up yourself.

8. Notice the pattern? Everything from C to A leaves the testing to you.

9. Claude plus Qube. S tier. You connect Qube to Claude and describe your strategy in plain English. Claude builds it in Qube, tests it on years of prices, and shows you every single trade. No code required.

10. The hard part of trading should be the idea, not learning how to code.
