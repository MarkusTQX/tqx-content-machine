## Script

1. Quant trading means no hunches. Rules and data make every call.

2. Full disclosure, I'm biased. We built something on this list.

3. ChatGPT for stock picks. F tier. Sounds super confident. Zero proof behind it.

4. Gemini. D tier. It's right inside Google, so it's great for a stock's latest news. But ask it to test a strategy, and it can spit out a profit number without running one trade. That number looks insane. Totally made up.

5. Grok. C tier. It lives on X, where a ton of traders hang out. So it knows what everyone's saying about a stock. But hype won't tell you if your strategy works.

6. Perplexity. B tier. Legit for research, and it shows you where every answer came from. But it won't test your strategy on past prices for you.

7. Claude. A tier. It's really strong at building strategies, and it'll even write the code. But testing that code on years of prices? You're on your own.

8. See the problem? Grok, Perplexity and Claude all dump the testing on you.

9. Claude plus Qube. S tier. Connect Qube to Claude and type your strategy like you're texting a friend. Claude builds it in Qube, tests it on years of prices, and shows you every trade. You never write a line of code.

10. Your job is the thinking of a strategy. The code shouldn't be your biggest obstacle.
