## Script

1. Claude's from Anthropic, Codex is from the ChatGPT people, and I connected Qube to both.

2. I typed this into each one. "Buy Apple when it's oversold, sell when it recovers."

3. Oversold means it dropped hard and fast. How hard? I didn't say.

4. Round one, the strategies. When to buy, when to sell, how much, when to get out.

5. Same sentence, two different strategies. Every word in yellow, I never typed.
   *Or, if they wrote the same strategy:* "Same sentence, same strategy, down to the numbers."

6. Round two, the backtests. Qube tests both on up to five years of real Apple prices.

7. Those yellow words gave me two different results.
   *Or, if they wrote the same strategy:* "The test came back the same, trade for trade."

8. Which one would I use? Claude. It sells if Apple keeps falling. Codex just holds on.
   *Or, if they differ most on how much:* "Which one would I use? Claude. It only puts in part of the cash. Codex goes all in."
   *Or, if they differ most on when to buy:* "Which one would I use? Claude. It waits for a real drop. Codex buys almost any dip."
   *Or, if they differ most on when to sell:* "Which one would I use? Claude. It knows when to take the win. Codex never says when."
   *Or, if they wrote the same strategy:* "Which one should you use? Honestly, whichever one you already use."

9. Either way, read the strategy your AI wrote before you trust the test.

10. The AI fills in everything you didn't say.

11. Comment QUBE and I'll send you the link for the free sign-up.
