## Hook formula and structure, and why
- **Hook formula:** A-vs-B question + personal experiment (`hook-writing` tips: "Personal experiment", "Is it possible…"; firm rule #8: lead with names people know: Claude and Codex, not Qube). Stake: "the better trading strategy". The second sentence makes the test fair and promises a result.
- **Structure:** shape 7, experiment/challenge (`story-structures`): the attempt (same sentence into both) → round 1 (the strategies) → round 2 (the backtests) → honest verdict → CTA. Two rounds give the viewer a scoreboard to follow, the way M05's tiers did.
- **Why:** it's the purest "same task given to several tools" comparison, with two names Priya already knows, and Qube in it honestly as the shared test bench both AIs connect to (Qube connects to Claude and Codex, `product-truth-qube`). Codex has only ever been a one-line "works too" mention in our videos; this is the first time it's the subject.
