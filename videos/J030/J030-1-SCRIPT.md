## Script

1. Think of it as three layers that need to integrate and work together.

2. Layer one is the brain. That's Claude, where you type your strategy in plain English.

3. Layer two is the testing layer. That's Qube. It builds your strategy and tests it on years of prices.

4. And the third layer is the execution layer. That's your broker, where the trades actually go.

5. I'd start with these three tools in paper trading, test out a few different strategies, and see how they perform.
