# Gold-standard scripts

**The bar comes from outside.** Our own videos haven't broken through yet (best: 2,610 views), so they can't be the standard. The gold scripts are Reels from other creators that did really well, picked by Mark. Our own posts are the baseline: every new script has to beat them, and get closer to the outside gold.

Rules say what good looks like. Gold scripts show it. Before drafting, read the outside gold closest to your format. Before handing a script over, compare it line by line: would it hold up next to that Reel?

When recreating a gold Reel, copy its hook word for word and write every other line fresh, following its beats and rhythm (`remix-for-product`, The method, Mark Oct 27), inside the 35–60s hard limit (`script-logic` #10). Never copy its footage or its body lines.

## Outside gold (the bar)

What gets in: a Reel Mark picks from another creator that clearly outperformed (far more views than the account's followers, or far above that creator's usual numbers). Each one is transcribed with `tools/study-video.sh` and broken down with `video-breakdown` first.

Each gold entry gets:
1. The numbers: views, likes, comments, shares/saves if visible, the creator's follower count, length.
2. A line-by-line table: time · line (as said) · on screen · what the line does (hook, setup, proof, re-hook, reveal, payoff, CTA) · why it works.
3. The rhythm: seconds per beat, where the re-hooks sit, where the payoff lands.
4. **The TQX move:** how each move would work in a GATC or Qube video, with a line written to our rules.

### Baseline 1 · M05 · "Quant trading tools, ranked S to F" (Mark, posted Oct 19)

Format: tier list, worst to best, product last. Persona: Daniel and Priya, with Marcus and Frank in the comments. Layout: talking head, tier board fixed in the top third and filling in as each tool lands, captions 2–4 words at a time.

| # | Time | Line (as said) | What works | Rule / skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | "I ranked 6 tools for building a trading strategy, from S to F, so you don't have to." | Format, count and payoff in one breath. "So you don't have to" promises a shortcut the video actually delivers. | Hook promise is paid off (`script-logic` #1) |
| 2 | 0:06 | "Quant trading just means trading with rules and data instead of your gut." | One plain sentence explains the only term a beginner needs, then moves on. | Explain a term the moment it appears (`spoken-sentence-rules` #8) |
| 3 | 0:10 | "And yes, I am biased, because we built one." | Owns the bias before Frank can call it out. Makes the ranking read as honest opinion. | Rankings marked as opinion (`finance-compliance`, `positioning`) |
| 4 | 0:13 | "Asking ChatGPT what to buy. F tier. You're just getting an opinion that nobody has actually tested." | Opens with the fight: the tool everyone uses lands at the bottom. The reason is one fair line. | Name tools freely, describe them accurately (`positioning`) |
| 5 | 0:19 | "Excel. D tier. You can test a strategy on past prices in a spreadsheet, but one wrong cell and your test is secretly using tomorrow's price to make today's trade. Your results look amazing, but they're fake." | Explains look-ahead bias with zero jargon, as a picture anyone with a spreadsheet can see. | Concrete, no vague nouns (`spoken-sentence-rules` #2, `trading-fundamentals`) |
| 6 | 0:33 | "MetaTrader. C tier. It's popular for forex, which is trading currencies like the dollar and the euro. But to build a bot that trades for you, it needs to learn its own coding language." | Explains forex inline. Sets up the pattern: needs code. | Every term explained inline |
| 7 | 0:44 | "TradingView. B tier. Really good charts, and it can test strategies on past prices. But to do that, you need to learn its coding language, Pine Script." | Gives credit first, then the catch. Fair to the tool, so the ranking feels earned. | Describe others accurately (`positioning`) |
| 8 | 0:53 | "QuantConnect. A tier. Very powerful for testing strategies, but you have to write real code in Python or C#." | Third "needs code" in a row. The viewer starts seeing the pattern before it's named. | Every line earns its place (`script-logic` #3) |
| 9 | 1:00 | "Notice the pattern? Everything from C to A wants you to be a programmer." | Re-hook at the 72% mark. Names the problem the reveal is about to solve. | One question, not a chain (`anti-ai-patterns` #6) |
| 10 | 1:04 | "Qube. S tier. You describe the idea in plain English. It turns it into exact rules, tests them on past prices, and runs it. No code required." | The reveal answers the exact pattern just named. Product only at the end, as a payoff, not a pitch. | Product in the last lines (`cta-and-offer`) |
| 11 | 1:13 | "The hard part of trading should be the idea, not learning how to code." | The point of the whole video in one line. It's TQX's difference, not a generic one. | You bring the idea, AI builds it (`positioning`) |
| 12 | 1:18 | "Disagree? Drop your tier list in the comments, or comment QUBE and I'll send you the full breakdown." | Two asks that work together: argue (comments) or take the tool (keyword). 41 comments. | Debate CTA before the keyword (`cta-and-offer`) |

**Rhythm:** one tool every 8–11 seconds. Each tool gets name → tier → one honest reason. The tier word is said on its own ("F tier."), which gives the edit a beat to hit.

**What to fix next time:**
- Line 6: "it needs to learn its own coding language". It's the *person* who needs to learn it, not MetaTrader. Fixed: "But to build a bot that trades for you, you have to learn its own coding language."
- Line 10: "and runs it". "It" has drifted from "the idea" to "the rules". Fixed: "...tests them on past prices, and runs them for you."
- Line 5 runs 34 words across two sentences joined by "but". It worked on camera, but it's over the 20-word guide (`spoken-sentence-rules` #9).

### Baseline 2 · M02 · "You lost to your own index fund" (Mark, posted Oct 15)

Format: common advice checked against a number. Persona: Daniel. Delivery: fast, no pauses.

| # | Line (as said) | Why it works / what's wrong | Rule / skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Everyone says just buy the S&P 500. In 2024, the average investor didn't even come close to it." | Starts from advice the viewer has heard, then breaks it with a year and a result. | Hook with a specific number or stake (`script-qa-checklist` Pass 7) |
| 2 | "You probably made less trading than just putting your money into a fund. And here's why." | Makes it about *their* money. This is why M02 had the second-best 3-second hold. | Open inside their world (`audience-profile`) |
| 3 | "'Just buy the S&P 500 and you'll make what the market makes.' You've heard this a thousand times." | Quotes the advice in the viewer's own words. | Audience language |
| 4 | "The S&P 500 is 500 of the biggest companies in America. An index fund that lets you buy all of them at once." | Explains the term. **Flaw:** the second sentence is a fragment. Fixed: "An index fund lets you buy all of them at once." | Every sentence ends (`spoken-sentence-rules` #1) |
| 5 | "In 2024, the S&P 500 went up by 25%. But the average American trader who owned stock funds only made 16.5%." | Two real numbers, same year. **Flaw:** line 1 says "investor", this says "trader". DALBAR measured fund *investors*; Frank will notice. Keep "investor" all the way through. | One term per thing (`terminology`); say what the source measured (`claims-and-proof`) |
| 6 | "Same market, same year. They got about two-thirds of the return. So where did the rest go?" | Rounds the number the way people talk (16.5 ÷ 25 = about two-thirds), then opens a loop. | Say numbers the way people say them (`spoken-sentence-rules` #7) |
| 7 | "It wasn't the fund. It was them." | The turn. Short, and it lands. (It's a real cause, not an "it's not X, it's Y" slogan.) | |
| 8 | "A research firm called DALBAR tracks this every year, and they found out that people buy and sell at the wrong times." | Names the source out loud, which builds trust with Frank. | Every number sourced (`claims-and-proof`) |
| 9 | "Picture a bus that goes exactly where you want to go. But every time the ride gets bumpy, you jump off and wait to catch the next one. You still get there, just later. And every single time." | The picture supports a real number instead of replacing it. **Flaw:** "And every single time." trails off. Fixed: "You still get there, just later. Every single time you jump off." | Picture holds, doesn't carry the video (`script-logic` #6); every sentence ends |
| 10 | "That's what people do with their funds. The market drops, so they sell. It goes back up, and they jump back in at a higher price." | Maps the bus straight back to the real behavior, step by step. | No skipped steps (`script-logic` #4) |
| 11 | "In 2025, people almost caught up with the market. But one good year does not fix the habit." | Uses the newer number honestly instead of hiding it. | Losses and inconvenient facts shown openly (`finance-compliance`) |
| 12 | "The fix is boring. Decide your rules for buying and selling before you start. Then stick to them, even when you're scared." | Honest, but **this is where M02 lost**: the viewer gets advice, not something to use. 0 saves. | Viewer gets something to use, try or compare (`script-qa-checklist` Pass 1) |
| 13 | "Comment QUBE and I'll send you a full breakdown on exactly how to do this." | Clear keyword CTA, but nothing before it showed Qube, so there's no reason to want it yet. 1 comment. | CTA earns its place (`cta-and-offer`) |

**What M05 had that M02 didn't:** a payoff the viewer can use. The fix for M02 would have landed on `positioning`'s "test it before you risk it": "Before you trade your own idea, test it on the last ten years and see if it even beats the S&P 500." That's something to try, with a reason to comment QUBE.

## How to use this when writing

1. **Before drafting:** pick the outside gold Reel closest to your format. Note its rhythm: seconds per beat, where the re-hook sits, where the payoff lands. Until the outside gold list exists, use the closest baseline (ranking → M05; myth checked against a number → M02; build → J016; series → J018), knowing it's the floor, not the target.
2. **While drafting:** each line should do the same job as the matching line in the gold Reel (hook, setup, proof, re-hook, reveal, payoff, ask).
3. **Before handing over:** read your script next to the gold Reel. If any line is weaker than its equivalent, rewrite it. It must also beat our baseline.

## Adding a new gold script

- **Outside gold:** Mark sends the link. Transcribe it (`tools/study-video.sh`), make the lesson card (`video-breakdown`), then add it here in the format above. Keep the 10 strongest; older ones move to `references/`.
- **Our own videos:** one of ours only joins the outside gold if it performs like one (far more views than our follower count). A **Big win** on `content-scorecard` that doesn't reach that level replaces a baseline entry instead.
- Every addition goes through `skill-updating`.
