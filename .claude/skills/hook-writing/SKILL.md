# Hook writing

Built Oct 23 2026 from 4 teaching creators (Devin/Nexus incl. his 50-template page, minolee, kienobifilms, adam.kelada.media), 43 template example videos and the 19 viral Reels Mark picked. Rule counts and sources: `content-machine/knowledge/rules/hooks.md`. Full 50-template table with a TQX line for each: `content-machine/knowledge/cards/teaching/hooks/2026-10-23_nexus_50-viral-hook-templates.md`.

## Every hook must (our rules, not optional)

1. **Make sense cold.** Someone who's never seen us, doesn't know our terms (`script-logic` #8).
2. **Carry a specific number, name or stake early** (`script-qa-checklist`).
3. **Be paid off** by a line you can point to (`script-logic` #1).
4. **Be one sentence, done by about 5 seconds** (measured median across 66 videos: 5.2s, about 12–20 words). The next line starts delivering on it, without giving the whole answer away (start, don't finish: `retention-writing` #2).
5. **Work in three layers at once:** the spoken line, the on-screen text, and the first frame. In the viral batch, the proof is on screen from frame 1 (the chart, the GitHub page, the finished result).
6. **Fit one persona** (`audience-profile`) and **pass `finance-compliance`.** No promised returns, ever.
7. **When recreating a viral Reel: one hook, the original's, word for word (Mark, Oct 23: "just use the same hook as the videos I send you", "only 1 hook per video").** No A/B/C options. The hook is the only part of the original we copy; every line after it is written fresh (`remix-for-product`, Mark, Oct 27). The original's hook includes the setup it shows on screen: if the spoken words need it to make sense cold (#1), say it or show it (J033 below).
8. **Older note, for non-recreations only: keep its hook.** Use its words, swapping only what isn't true for us. If the original leads with a name people know (Claude, ChatGPT, Micron), keep that name; don't swap it for Qube, which nobody knows yet (Mark's note on J022). If the video is about Qube itself, Qube can go in the hook (Mark, Oct 23). Judge each video; this isn't a blanket ban.

## Firm rules (taught by 3+ creators)

### 1. Promise a hidden truth
"What no one told you", "the reality of X", "what they don't want you to know". (devinjatho, minolee, kienobifilms)

| Bad | Fixed (TQX) |
|---|---|
| "Let's talk about how hedge funds trade." | "Here's what hedge funds don't want you to know about trading bots." |
| "Some facts about backtests." | "I really hope I'm not the first person to tell you this, but your backtest is probably lying to you." |

Pay it off with a real, sourced truth (`claims-and-proof`). The "secret" must be true and specific, never vague hype.

### 2. Warn them before an action
"Watch this before you X", "Never start X without Y", "Whatever you do, don't forget Z". (devinjatho, kienobifilms, adam.kelada.media)

| Bad | Fixed (TQX) |
|---|---|
| "Paper trading is a good idea." | "For the love of god, whatever you do before your first live trade, do not forget to paper trade." |
| "Some tips for building bots." | "Watch this before you build a trading bot with ChatGPT." |
| "Things to check in a strategy." | "Never trade a strategy if you haven't done these 3 checks." |

Fits Daniel and Kai best: it lands right before the mistake they're about to make.

### 3. Put a short timeframe in the hook
The video's ("in 60 seconds", "in the next 45 seconds") or the result's ("in under a minute", "after just one hour"). (devinjatho, adam.kelada.media, minolee)

| Bad | Fixed (TQX) |
|---|---|
| "Here's how to backtest." | "Everyone tells you to backtest your trading idea, but nobody shows you how. Here's a 45-second tutorial you can save." |
| "I built a trading bot." | "Watch me build a trading bot in 60 seconds. No code." (J016) |
| "Here's what quant firms know." | "It took quant firms decades to figure this out. Here are their 3 most important rules in 45 seconds." |

The timeframe is a promise: the video has to actually deliver in that time (`script-logic` #1).

## Tips to try (taught by 1–2 creators)

Use them freely; they're not required. Each has a TQX line ready to adapt.

| Tip | TQX line |
|---|---|
| Tell them they're doing it wrong / the popular fix won't work | "I don't know who needs to hear this, but your trading bot isn't working because your backtest is lying to you." |
| Squeeze years into seconds | "Here's 30 years of stock market research, summarized in 60 seconds." 🔢 |
| "Everyone tells you X, but no one shows you Y" | "Everyone says 'test your strategy'. Nobody shows you how to tell luck from skill." |
| Personal experiment | "I let an AI paper trade $10,000 for 30 days. Here's what happened." (badge: paper trading) |
| Explain it like they're 5 / let me simplify | "Wall Street gatekeeps backtesting by making it sound complicated. So let me simplify it." |
| Confession | "I shouldn't be giving this away for free, but here's how to turn any trading idea into a tested strategy in under 60 seconds with Qube." |
| Call out the viewer | "If you trade on your phone and you're down this year, this video will either piss you off or make your day." |
| Specific outcome + "exactly what to do" | "If I woke up tomorrow down 50% on my account, here's exactly what I'd do to stop the bleeding." |
| Numbered list with a promise | "Top 3 AI trading tools you're sleeping on. Wake up." (ranking = opinion) |
| "Don't waste your time, here are the 3 that work" | "We've run hundreds of backtests. Don't waste your time. Here are the 3 checks that actually matter." 🔢 |
| One unusual power word | "disgustingly easy", "stupidly simple", not "super easy", "ultimate hack", "secret strategy" |
| "People are beginning to realize…" | "People are beginning to realize that most trading bots are just one lucky backtest." |
| "Is it possible to [big result] in [tiny time]?" | "Is it possible to build and test a trading strategy in 15 minutes with no code?" |
| A stat, "but here's what they didn't tell you" | "A study of 19,646 Brazilian day traders found 97% of the long-run ones lost money. But here's what they didn't tell you." 🔢 |
| Reframe what they chase | "Do you want a hot tip? Or do you want rules you've actually tested?" |

🔢 = the number must be true and in `claims-and-proof`.

## Rejected (breaks our rules)

| Formula | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| "If you do X every day, you'll make A, B, C" (money) | Promised result (`finance-compliance`) | A labelled test: "This rule ran every day for 10 years. Here's what it did." |
| "How to turn $50 into $5,000" | Promised return | "How to turn one trading idea into ten tested versions." |
| "[Strategies] that rarely fail" | Implies safe returns | "3 strategies quants have studied for decades, so you can steal their homework." |
| "It's not X, it's Y" contrast hooks | `anti-ai-patterns` #1 | Say the second half directly. |
| Teases that are never paid off | `script-logic` #1 | Only promise what the video delivers. |

## The process: 5 hooks per script

Presenters record several hooks back to back (`tqx-voice`), so every script gets 5.

1. Write the outline first (`script-logic`): know the promise and the payoff.
2. Write **5 hooks using 5 different mechanisms**: at least two firm rules, plus three from the tips or the viral openings.
3. For each, write the on-screen text (8 words or fewer, `on-screen-text`) and the first frame.
4. Check each against "Every hook must" above. Cut any that fail.
5. Rank the top 3 and say why in one line each. The body's first line must work after any of them.

```
HOOKS
1. [spoken] · On screen: "[...]" · First frame: [...] · Mechanism: [firm 1 / tip / viral] · Persona: [...]
...
Top 3: [#, #, #]: [one line why]
```

## Checks

- [ ] 5 hooks, 5 different mechanisms, at least 2 firm.
- [ ] Each makes sense cold, has a number/name/stake, and is done by about 5 seconds.
- [ ] Spoken line, on-screen text and first frame all planned.
- [ ] Every number is in `claims-and-proof`; no promised result.
- [ ] The payoff line for each hook exists in the script.
- [ ] No worn power words ("ultimate hack", "secret strategy", "game-changer").
