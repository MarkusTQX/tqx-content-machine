# TQX audience profile

Source: "TQX User Personas (Hypotheses)", Oct 6 2026. These replace the older Jack/Joe matrix from the Oct 6 content meeting. They are hypotheses: test them against real comments and data, and update this file when the data disagrees.

This skill describes WHO is watching. It does not decide what videos to make. Ideas come from the videos Mark finds; this file only tells you how to land them for a specific person.

## The one rule that matters most

Intent beats technical skill. Someone who badly needs to fix a problem sticks around; someone who is merely curious drifts off. For low-intent personas (Priya, Frank, Kai) the job is to raise intent: give them a goal, a stake, or something to try today.

## The personas

### Daniel, the burned retail trader (high intent, no code)
- **Who:** Trades stocks, options or CFDs on his phone around a day job. Has done it for a year or more. Down overall.
- **Already knows:** What a stock, a chart and a stop-loss are. Has heard "most traders lose".
- **Doesn't know:** How to test an idea before risking money. What a backtest really is.
- **Pain:** Overtrades, sells winners early, holds losers, chases tips. Can't code.
- **Burned by:** Tip groups, a pretty backtest that lost money live, slow setups.
- **What lands:** Research on why retail traders lose; rules plus honest testing; tools he can use without code.
- **What loses him:** Get-rich bots, overpromises, anything slow to set up.
- **His line:** "I know what I should do. I just don't do it once the trade is open."

### Priya, the AI-curious tinkerer (low intent, light tech)
- **Who:** Uses ChatGPT or Claude at work. Casual app investor.
- **Already knows:** How to prompt an AI chatbot.
- **Doesn't know:** Trading jargon. Drawdown and slippage are a wall.
- **Pain:** Afraid of wasting money on a toy.
- **What lands:** AI vs AI as a sport; explanations that make it click; "let the AI run it and see what happens".
- **What loses her:** Novelty fading; dull first results.
- **Her line:** "I just want to see what happens if I let an AI run it for a month."

### Marcus, the quant competitor (strong tech)
- **Who:** Writes Python. Has used Quantopian, Kaggle or WorldQuant BRAIN.
- **Already knows:** Backtesting, factors, Sharpe ratio, overfitting. Do not explain these to him.
- **Pain:** Opaque scoring; backtests that broke live.
- **What lands:** Exact mechanics; GATC's 55% return / 45% risk scoring; insider detail.
- **What loses him:** Vague rules; nothing at stake.
- **His line:** "Show me exactly how the risk score is calculated."

### Ethan, the career builder (student or recent grad in HK, SG or the US)
- **Who:** Wants something on his CV that recruiters respect.
- **Already knows:** Some finance or CS from school.
- **Pain:** Top firms are almost impossible to get into (Citadel: under 1% of 69,000 intern applicants got in, 2023).
- **What lands:** Quant gatekeeping ("can you answer this?"), the career angle, tools ranked side by side.
- **What loses him:** No visible credential; exam season.
- **His line:** "If I place in GATC, will a recruiter actually care?"

### Frank, the skeptic (not a user yet)
- **Who:** Experienced manual trader or investor. Active in comments.
- **Already knows:** A lot. He spots a slightly wrong explanation instantly.
- **Depth:** Don't explain basics to him (backtest, stop-loss, drawdown). Get every detail exactly right, and show the source and the "Backtest, not live results" label instead.
- **Burned by:** Signal services and scam bots.
- **What lands:** Scam-spotting; losses shown as openly as wins; "Backtest, not live results" labels.
- **What loses him:** Any overpromise. His comments set the tone for everyone else reading.
- **His line:** "If it worked, why would they sell it to me?"

### Kai, the Gen Z AI native (18 to 24)
- **Who:** Trades crypto; uses Polymarket and Kalshi; learns on TikTok, X and Discord.
- **Pain:** Memecoin swings, liquidations, fees.
- **What lands:** AI vs AI spectacle, "which type are you" sorting, fast tempo, scam-spotting.
- **What loses him:** Slow results, friction, bigger hype somewhere else.
- **His line:** "Can I just tell the AI what I think will happen and let it trade?"

## How to use this when writing

1. Name one persona per script. Write it at the top of the script notes.
2. Open inside their world: their app, their pain, their words. The hook should sound like their line, or answer it.
3. Pitch at their level. Explain what they don't know; never explain what they already know.
4. Use only examples they live with: a day job, a phone, Telegram tips, ChatGPT, memecoins, recruiting season.

| Bad (generic) | Fixed (Daniel) |
|---|---|
| "Traders often struggle with emotional decision-making." | "You know you should sell. Then the trade's open and you just... don't." |

| Bad (explains what Marcus knows) | Fixed (Marcus) |
|---|---|
| "A backtest means testing your strategy on past prices." | "55% of your GATC score is return. 45% is risk. Here's how the risk half is calculated." |
