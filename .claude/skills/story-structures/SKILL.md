# Story structures

Built Oct 23 2026 from 62 videos that did well: 43 example videos behind the hook templates (`knowledge/cards/structure/`) and the 19 viral Reels Mark picked (`knowledge/cards/reference/`). Full counts: `content-machine/knowledge/rules/structure-patterns.md`.

Only one creator taught structure, so these are scored by **how many successful videos do it** (Mark, Oct 23): standard = more than half, common = a quarter to a half, option = fewer.

## The timing every shape follows (standard)

| Beat | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to about 5s | One sentence (`hook-writing`) |
| First move toward the answer | Right after the hook | Start answering at once (first step, first item, first fact). Credibility, if any, is one line. |
| The middle | 5s to about 75% | Numbered items or steps, each with one proof on screen. A re-hook around 60–75% (`retention-writing`). |
| Full payoff | About **80%** of the length (middle half of videos: 66–88%) | The thing the hook promised, complete |
| End | Within a few seconds of the payoff | The CTA or one closing line. No recap (`anti-ai-patterns` #9). |

**Start, don't finish.** Begin answering straight away, but hold the *full* answer back until about 80%. Giving the whole answer at the start means "they had no reason to stay" (@viewcci).

| Bad | Fixed |
|---|---|
| "The secret is out-of-sample testing. Here's what that means..." (whole answer in line 2) | "Most backtests fall apart live. There are three checks that catch it, and the third is the one almost nobody runs." (starts, holds the best for last) |

## The 8 shapes

Pick one per video. Each comes with beats, where it's been seen, a TQX version and the persona it suits.

### 1. Steps / how-to (17 of 62: the most common)
**Beats:** hook (promise + timeframe) → "let me show you" → step 1, 2, 3 (each on screen) → result → CTA
**Variant, "new power"** (6 viral Reels; best fit for TQX): "Claude can now…" → name the tools in under 8s → numbered parts, one job each, real screen each → "here's where it gets crazy" → result → keyword for the guide
**TQX:** "Claude can now build and backtest your trading idea in Qube." Steps: type the idea → Qube writes the rules → backtest, every trade → improve → paper trade.
**Persona:** Priya, Kai · **Seen in:** ai.with.andrew, samin_yasar12, julias.algos (Hedgia), max_kelleyy; tpl22, tpl23, tpl28, tpl38, tpl47

### 2. Numbered or ranked list (13 of 62)
**Beats:** hook (count + promise) → item 1 … item N, each "name → one reason → proof on screen" → best item last, said as "the most important" → CTA or closing line
**TQX:** M05's tier list; "3 checks before you trust a backtest"; "4 strategies you can test in Qube without code" (the 4 templates on the Qube page).
**Persona:** Daniel, Priya · **Seen in:** M05 (ours), thecodingjesusquant, shelby.sapp, raycfu (10 ways); tpl03, tpl08, tpl39, tpl42, tpl44, tpl49, tpl50

### 3. Problem → solution (9 of 62)
**Beats:** hook (the pain, or "the reason you can't…") → why it happens (one cause) → what most people try → the fix (product or method) shown → result → CTA
**TQX:** "Why is ChatGPT so bad at trading ideas?" → it gives opinions, not tests → Claude connected to Qube tests the idea → labelled result.
**Persona:** Daniel, Priya · **Seen in:** aiwithremy, raycfu (loop), julias.algos (Codex); tpl15, tpl19, tpl27, tpl32, tpl36, tpl48

### 4. Myth vs truth (6 of 62)
**Beats:** contrarian hook ("You don't need X", "DO NOT do X") → "let me explain" → the old belief → why it backfires (a number) → the truth → a closing rule
**TQX:** "You don't need to code to build a trading bot." · "DO NOT build one AI trading bot. Build a team of agents." (GATC)
**Persona:** Daniel, Frank, Marcus · **Seen in:** max_kelleyy; M02 (ours); tpl02, tpl12, tpl16, tpl18, tpl34

### 5. Shock number → the opposite → why (7 of 62)
**Beats:** absurd number from a simple rule → "now imagine the exact opposite" → named source on screen → "but why?" → the explanation (two groups, one cause) → share CTA or test it
**TQX:** "If you'd bought the S&P 500 at every close and sold at every open…" → test the rule live in Qube → labelled result, costs included.
**Persona:** Daniel · **Seen in:** markvictorn, joe.investss, svermm (same topic, 3 accounts); tpl01, tpl11, tpl43

### 6. Comparison (4 of 62)
**Beats:** "the one thing [winners] do differently" → group A → group B → group C → the rule
**TQX:** "Here's what quant firms do completely differently from retail traders": retail (tips, gut) → serious amateurs (charts) → quants (rules, tests, risk limits).
**Persona:** Ethan, Daniel · **Seen in:** tpl26, tpl33, tpl40, tpl46

### 7. Experiment / challenge (4 of 62)
**Beats:** "Day X of…" or "I tried X for Y" + a visible clock → the attempt → time skip → the result with an honest reaction → CTA
**TQX:** "Day 1: can I build a trading strategy in 15 minutes with no code?" (Qube, labelled backtest, wins and losses). Or the paper-trading series (J018).
**Persona:** Kai · **Seen in:** copyandrea; tpl06, tpl07, tpl41

### 8. Explain it simply (2 of 62)
**Beats:** open on the finished result → "explained for 5-year-olds" → one rule per beat, drawn on screen → loop back to the first frame
**TQX:** a famous day-trading rule typed into Qube and backtested, every trade and the drawdown shown.
**Persona:** Daniel, Priya · **Seen in:** erickjablonski; tpl09

## Choosing a shape

| If the video is… | Use |
|---|---|
| Showing Qube, Claude/Codex or GATC doing something | 1 (new power variant) |
| Comparing tools, rules or mistakes | 2 |
| Fixing a pain the viewer feels | 3 |
| Breaking a belief | 4 |
| Built on a surprising number | 5 |
| About who wins and who loses | 6 |
| A series or a personal test | 7 |
| A rule that sounds complicated | 8 |
| A recreation of a viral Reel | Keep the original's shape (`remix-for-product`) |

## Checks

- [ ] One shape, named in the outline.
- [ ] Hook by about 5s; the first move toward the answer comes right after it.
- [ ] The full payoff lands at about 65–90% of the length.
- [ ] Lists: the count is announced and the best item comes last.
- [ ] The end comes within a few seconds of the payoff. No recap.
- [ ] Length per `script-logic` #10.
