# Slop: what we've already made

"Slop" is Mark's name for content that's quick to produce at volume: well written, but anyone could post it. This file records every topic we've written so far, so the machine knows what's been done and how it performed.

Don't use this list to pick new topics. New ideas come from the hot videos Mark finds (see `video-breakdown` and `remix-for-product`). Use this file only to:
1. Check that a new idea isn't a repeat.
2. Pull a quick volume video when Mark asks for one.
3. Learn from the performance notes below.

## Why most of it is slop (Mark, Oct 21 2026)

- **Anyone could post it.** "What AI trading is", "the 6 steps", "strategies expire": general explainers any finance account could make.
- **The metaphor does all the work.** Spam filter, restaurant, free throws, shortcut home. A picture instead of a real moment, result or tool.
- **No real proof.** No real Qube run, no GATC leaderboard, no real trade. The paper-trading week was scripted.
- **Nothing to take away.** The viewer learns a fact but gets nothing to use, try or compare.

## Performance so far (Mark, Oct 21 2026)

- Posted: J001–J009 and M01–M06.
- **No Joshua video has passed 200 views.**
- **Only two Mark videos have passed 500 views:**
  - **M05 "Quant trading tools, ranked S to F"**, "by a landslide". It ranks real tools people use, picks a fight (ChatGPT in F tier), and hands the viewer a tool to try.
  - **M02, the S&P 500 video** ("You lost to your own index fund"; confirmed in Metricool, posted Oct 15). It's relatable: it's about the viewer's own money and their own index fund.
- Mark's read: be relatable, and give people a tool they can actually go and use. Stop explaining quant trading with basic metaphors.

The two winners are **not** slop. They're the bar.
