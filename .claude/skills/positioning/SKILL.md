# Positioning

Every TQX video pushes back on one enemy and lands on one difference. Keep the enemy and the difference to one each per video. Name the enemy only when it helps; the difference has to be clear by the end.

## The enemies (Mark, Oct 22)

| Enemy | What it looks like | Who's been hurt by it (`audience-profile`) | Proof we can use (`claims-and-proof`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tip groups and gurus** | Telegram signals, "copy my trades", paid Discords, influencer picks | Daniel, Frank | Robinhood users buying the most-talked-about stocks did worse (Barber et al. 2022) |
| **Get-rich bots and scams** | "$89 into $68,024", "10% a month", bots that can't lose | Frank, Kai, Daniel | CFTC: one bot scheme took $1.7B from 23,000+ people; "AI technology can't predict the future" |
| **Trading on gut feeling** | Overtrading, selling winners early, holding losers, panic-selling | Daniel, Kai | 97% of Brazil's long-run day traders lost money; heavy traders earned 11.4% vs the market's 17.9% |
| **Wall Street gatekeeping** | Quant firms have the data, the tools and the PhDs; retail gets an app and a chart | Ethan, Marcus, Priya | Two Sigma's 10,000+ data sources; Citadel took under 1% of 69,000 intern applicants |

## The three differences (Mark, Oct 22: all three are ours)

| Difference | In one spoken line | Product proof (`product-truth-qube`, `product-truth-gatc`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Test it before you risk it** | "Test your idea on years of past prices before you put a cent of real money on it." | Qube backtests and shows every trade; GATC agents run on a simulation account; losses shown as openly as wins |

## Naming other tools (Mark, Oct 22)

- **Name them freely.** Tools people already know (Claude, Codex, ChatGPT, TradingView, Robinhood, QuantConnect, MetaTrader, Excel) spark curiosity and keep people watching. Use them in builds, how-tos and news, not just rankings. Example: "Watch me build a trading bot with Claude."
- **Rank them openly.** Like M05: describe each one from its own website, mark the ranking as opinion, and own the bias. "And yes, I'm biased. We built one."
- **Describe them accurately.** Say what a tool actually does. "ChatGPT can't test your idea on past prices on its own" is fair if true. "ChatGPT is useless for trading" is not.
- **Never call a company or a person a scam** unless a regulator or court has. Call out the *pattern* instead (`finance-compliance`).

## Comparisons we never make

| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| "Qube makes more money than [tool or fund]" | No promised returns; backtests aren't live results |
| Our backtest vs someone else's live results | Not the same thing; Frank will call it out |
| Our results vs a guru's or a bot's claimed results | Their claims are unverified |
| "Unlike [named company], we're not a scam" | Implies they are one |
| "Better than a hedge fund" | We give people tools; we don't beat funds |
| "AI that picks stocks for you" | Not what Qube is. The person brings the idea (`product-truth-qube`) |
| Calling GATC a way to make money | It's a competition; never imply anyone will win prize money |
