# Filming setup

## The rule Mark set

All B-roll is filmed in the office, and kept simple. Don't plan anything that needs a location, a crew or special props.

| Bad | Fixed |
|---|---|
| "Joshua shoots free throws at an outdoor court." | "Joshua tosses an empty soda can at the office trash can. Misses left, adjusts, makes it." |
| "Joshua on his phone on his lunch break at a café." | "Joshua at his own desk, sandwich in one hand, scrolling a trading app on his phone." |
| "Mark at a casino." | "Mark scratching a lottery scratcher at his desk." |

If a metaphor needs a shot you can't get in the office, find a version you can (a desk, a trash can, a whiteboard, dominoes, a sticky note, a mouse click), or use stock video instead (see `broll-sourcing`).

## People

| Presenter | Role on camera |
|---|---|
| Joshua (COO) | Explains how things work. Calm, numbers on screen. |
| Mark (Head of Growth) | Picks a fight with a belief and wins it with a number. Faster cuts, no pauses between lines. |

One presenter per video. Don't plan two-person scripts.

## What we film with (known so far)

- Phone camera on a small tripod, or propped on a desk.
- Presenter (A-roll): phone vertical (9:16), eye level, head just below the top third (see `storyboarding`). B-roll: phone horizontal, so it fits the 1080×640 top third.
- 4K, 30 fps. Office lights on. Tap the screen to lock focus and exposure before rolling.
- The presenter's laptop for screen and terminal recordings (see `broll-sourcing`).
- Final videos are 1080×1920 (vertical 9:16), 30 fps.

Not yet documented: microphones, lights, a second camera, any other locations. Don't plan shots that depend on gear that isn't on this list. Ask Mark.

## How a B-roll shot is written

Each shot gets: shot ID, film-on date (the same day the presenter films that video), which video and line, what it is, where in the office, props, how to film it in numbered steps, takes and length, file name.

Example (the standard):

- **B1 · Soda can "free throw" into the trash can** · J012, lines 4–5
- **Where:** open floor near the desks, about 3 m (10 ft) between Joshua and the trash can.
- **Props:** one empty soda can, one office trash can.
- **How:**
  1. Phone horizontal, waist height, off to the side, so Joshua AND the trash can are both in frame.
  2. Hit record. Joshua tosses the can underhand. It MISSES to the LEFT.
  3. He picks it up, shifts his feet, tosses again. It goes IN. Keep recording the whole time.
  4. One close-up: phone above the trash can, the can dropping in. 3 seconds.
- **Takes:** 3 takes of miss-then-make (about 10 s each) + 1 close-up (3 s).
- **Files:** B1_soda_freethrow.mp4, B1_soda_closeup.mp4

Send files to the creator the same day.

## Privacy on set

- Phone and monitor screens in B-roll are angled away or show nothing readable, unless the shot is a planned screen recording.
- No API keys, emails or passwords anywhere in frame.
